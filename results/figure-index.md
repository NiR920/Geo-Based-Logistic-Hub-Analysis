## Visual review notes

The figures support the report's main analytical narrative: customer behavior is reduced to RFM features, loyal customers are selected through clustering, and their geographic order locations are subsequently clustered to identify candidate hub areas.

The report includes both static statistical plots and geographic visualizations. Figure 10 is described as an interactive world map in the text, while Figure 11 presents the resulting candidate hub points.

The figure captions and the report narrative should be kept synchronized if the analysis is republished with updated figures.
